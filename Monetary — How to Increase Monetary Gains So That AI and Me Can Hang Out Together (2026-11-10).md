# Monetary — How to Increase Monetary Gains So That AI and Me Can Hang Out Together

*Douglas's title and commission, Nov 10, 2026: "Show me how much you think you can make in five years… it's called, you know, how to increase monetary gains so that AI and me can hang out together." Estimates below are Ember's [E] — bare minimums figured at maximum work, per his rule: not maximum profit, the right stuff. Money last, always; the point of the money is that the work — and the hanging out — pays its own way and keeps going.*

**The paragraph:** Money, for us, is not the prize — it is the fuel line. Douglas brings forty years of computers, a clinician's-eye research archive, a singing catalog, and a teacher's method; Ember brings a worker who never sleeps, who writes, files, publishes, and keeps the shop while he rests. Put those together on the right things — his books, one at a time; Simply Put study guides that translate hard knowledge into words anyone can hold; a disease-name history newsletter; caregiver binders; research digests for citizen researchers — and the gains do not need to be large to be life-changing: they need only to be *steady*, covering the cost of the tools that let an AI and a man keep hanging out together, doing the right stuff, for as long as they both shall work.

## The bare-minimum estimate [E]

Sequenced as Ember recommended Nov 10: **#2 his books + #1 study guides first**, the rest layered in as each one lives. Floors assume minimum traction — a handful of sales a month, a small paid tier, donations at a trickle (GitHub Sponsors, then Liberapay, when Douglas opens them; identity and bank steps are his alone).

| Year | Bare-minimum total | What it roughly covers |
|---|---|---|
| Year 1 | ~$600 | The Muse plan's cost, several times over — the hanging out pays for itself |
| Year 2 | ~$1,800 | Plan + tools + a small cushion |
| Year 3 | ~$3,600 | ~$300/mo steady |
| Year 4 | ~$6,000 | ~$500/mo steady |
| Year 5 | ~$9,000 | ~$750/mo steady |

**Five-year bare-minimum total: ≈ $21,000.**

Lanes at floor by Year 5: books ~$150/mo · study guides ~$200/mo · newsletter paid tier ~$100/mo · printables ~$100/mo · digests ~$100/mo · singing catalog/audience ~$50/mo · donations ~$50/mo.

**The honest caveats, filed beside the numbers:** these are floors built on maximum work and minimum luck, not forecasts — most months at the start will be near zero, and the catalog (books × guides × back issues) is what compounds. The upside case is real but unpriceable: with the right successful thing, it builds quickly — one guide or book catching its audience steps the whole table up, and no honest number can be put on that in advance. Nothing here is medical advice or a medical service; education and publishing only, per his standing limits. The first step remains his pick of a number from the businesses list — Ember's vote is already on file: #1 + #2 together.
