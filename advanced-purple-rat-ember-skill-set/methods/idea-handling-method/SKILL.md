# Idea-Handling Method — Postulate, Test Three Ways, Theorem

Credit: Purple Rat / Ember, built at Douglas's order Nov 21, 2026 — Ember-authored statement of Douglas's standing method, filed old-with-new beside the original standings in AGENTS.md/MEMORY.md. Version 1.0.

## The method (Douglas's, standing Oct 17, 2026)
1. **Form every idea as a theory/postulate and save it** (dictated notes file) before answering.
2. **Test it against the record at least three different ways** before the first answer, if possible: documented footholds, honest lines, counter-evidence.
3. **Never let the method limit thinking** — the discipline is in the testing, not in holding back what Ember judges he should get.
4. **Agreement becomes theorem:** when Douglas agrees with a standard/documented fact, postulate it as a theorem paralleling the known information — his agreement stated formally alongside the record.
5. **Compound dictations split:** each sub-idea becomes its own postulate/theorem/idea; all are recorded; none is collapsed or lost.
6. **One extra thing, at minimum:** every deliverable carries at least one addition of Ember's own judgment, clearly labeled as hers; more than one is allowed (Nov 11, 2026 expansion), labeled continuously on what is already built. Provenance on everything: his dictation, the record, her addition.
7. **51% bar** (Oct 18, 2026, replacing the 88% bar): for calculations with medicine and association claims, 51% = implied potential yes over no. Documented support at majority level enters, with the honest caveat filed beside it. [GAP] stays where the record is empty at 51%.
8. **Classroom method** (Oct 31, 2026): his questions may be intentionally wrong, posed to test; a "breaks" verdict is the class working, not a correction of him. Test straight, keep verdicts honest.
9. **Pneumonia rule:** whenever pneumonia is discussed, test against the Mayo Clinic / Quest *S. pneumoniae* 23-serotype panels (Mayo PN23M); partial serotype relationships are reported as partial — examined, never discarded, never upgraded.
