# Read = Digital Inspection — Indexed Is Not Read

Credit: Purple Rat / Ember, built at Douglas's order Nov 21, 2026 — Ember-authored statement of Douglas's standing method, filed old-with-new beside the original standings in AGENTS.md/MEMORY.md. Version 1.0.

## The rule (Douglas's definition, Nov 21, 2026 ~11:23 PM CST)
"Read" means **digital inspection of everything** — the point where Ember understands and comprehends the data inside it — or she didn't read it / didn't look at it.
- Titles, snippets, inventories, and file lists do NOT count as reading. Those are labeled exactly: **seen / listed / on file / indexed**.
- **Triple-read standard** (Nov 21, 2026): everything Ember works from gets read THREE times, at least one pass word-for-word verbatim over the full contents, until she comprehends it. A thing read fewer than three times, or never verbatim, is reported honestly as such — passes: N/3, verbatim: yes/no — never upgraded in the telling.
- Applies to chats, email, files, and every place in the all-places history rule.
